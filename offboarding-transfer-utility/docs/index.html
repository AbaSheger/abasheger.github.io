Access and requirements

Offboarding Transfer Utility is a Jira administration page. A Jira administrator opens Jira settings, selects Apps, and then opens Offboarding Transfer Utility.

Jira administrator

The invoking user must have Jira's global administrator permission. The App verifies this permission for every user-invoked operation.

Licensed access

An active Atlassian Marketplace subscription or a valid trial is required to scan, transfer, view history, or delete history.

If the App displays License required, ask a site administrator to start a trial or activate the subscription, then refresh the page.

The three-step workflow

1. Select departing user

Search for and select the Jira user who is leaving. The App scans the supported Jira categories and presents the asset footprint for review.

2. Choose successor

Select the Jira user who should receive ownership or open-issue assignments. The successor must be different from the departing user.

3. Review and transfer

Review the departing user, successor, and category totals in the confirmation dialog. The App does not begin changes until the administrator confirms the transfer.

Result

The completion state reports successful and failed counts by category. Review any item-level errors before retrying or making manual Jira changes.

Supported Jira assets

The App scans and transfers the following categories when they are associated with the selected departing user:

  • Filters: ownership of supported Jira filters.
  • Dashboards: ownership of supported Jira dashboards.
  • Project leads: the lead assigned to Jira projects.
  • Component leads: the lead assigned to Jira project components.
  • Open assigned issues: open Jira issues currently assigned to the departing user.

Jira applies its own permissions and product rules to every change. An individual item can fail even when other categories complete successfully.

Administrator-only transfer history

Transfer history is available only to licensed Jira administrators. The App stores one audit record for each completed transfer run in Atlassian Forge storage. A record identifies the administrator, departing user, successor, timestamp, category counts, outcomes, and bounded error details.

180-day retention: audit records expire automatically 180 days after creation. A Jira administrator can also delete all retained transfer history immediately from the App.

Asset details found during a scan are transient and are not retained as a separate inventory. For the complete data-handling description, read the Privacy Policy.

Review and transfer safety

The App cannot automatically undo a transfer. Confirm the selected users and asset totals carefully. A partial result means successful items may already have changed even when other items failed.

After a partial failure, review the reported category and error, then rescan the departing user before deciding whether a retry is appropriate.
